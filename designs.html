Proprietary Safety Architectures

Four advanced impact-absorbing designs engineered to shift hardware states under high-velocity vector windows [17/501,355].

Connected Sections Model

Features independent, interlocking dark gray composite tile blocks that monitor force telemetry signatures. Upon an active impact calculation window, adjacent lateral connection link pins immediately disengage, allowing individual targeted module cells to lower vertically while surrounding sections maintain structural rigidity.

  • Sub-millisecond microprocessor-controlled separation loops [17/501,355]
  • Solenoid-driven horizontal locking bolt fasteners [17/501,355]
  • Mechanical interlock free-fall load attenuation panels [17/501,355]

Trampoline Sub-Floor Model

Integrates a matrix of high-speed motorized jointed robotic arms and micro-stepper motor linkages hidden underneath a high-strength athletic canvas. Sensors track incoming fall trajectories to execute a synchronized structural pull loop, drawing a suspended rigid panel and membrane downward right before a kinetic load impacts.
